Volunteer can also be a donor

  • A volunteer is a role on the same person record as a donor, so there is one record per person.
  • “Related to a donor” uses the existing Relationships tab (spouse, parent, employer).
  • Funds a volunteer raises are credited to them as fundraiser; their own gifts stay as donor giving — never double-counted.
  • Under-18 volunteers link to a parent or guardian, whose consent is stored.

Best practices built in

  • Contact within 48 hours and a first shift within 30 days.
  • Matching by interests, skills, availability and distance.
  • Self-service portal: shifts, hours, fundraising, profile.
  • Automatic reminders and thank-yous; QR check-in for hours.
  • Recognition: milestones, Volunteer Honor Roll, Action Center awards.
  • Corporate volunteer grants from verified hours.

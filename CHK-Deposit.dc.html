Donor's own check or bill-pay → CRY tax receipt.Fiduciary — donor-advised fund, family fund, CAF America / CyberGrants, Benevity → gift credited to the recommending donor or employee, thank-you only; the fiduciary has receipted. Fiduciaries are flagged on their organisation record, so this is automatic.Company paying a match directly → credited to the company, acknowledgement to the company.Interest and other non-donation deposits (e.g. Merrill Lynch interest) stay in QuickBooks and are not brought into the CRM.

WF-16 · Campaign Master: set up, match gifts, roll up
Every gift belongs to one campaign. Staff set the campaign up once with a link for each source; imports then find the campaign by that link. Mailers, EDMs, call lists and pledges hang off the campaign as child records, and the campaign shows their totals.

Points to confirm

  • Ringing and Voicemail rule is read as written: a call on Friday moves to Saturday; a call on any other day moves to the next Friday. If you meant “next working day”, a Wednesday call would move to Thursday instead.
  • Max attempts = how many times a donor can get the same status before leaving the queue (e.g. Ringing 3 times, then email and stop).
  • Email triggers also respect the Do Not Email (DNE) flag in the donor record, not just whether an email exists.
  • Calling hours are 9 AM–8 PM in the donor's time zone, worked out from the State (the sheet's time-zone column is empty).
